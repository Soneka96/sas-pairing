# P3 conformance cases

> These are documented conformance cases for experimental implementation work, not executable tests or production approval. The owner selected the remote vodozemac profile for experimental implementation. “Reject before exposure” means before release of the role's SAS-enabling ephemeral public contribution.

> **CASE STATUS:** `R-OWNER-*`, `R-WIRE-*`, and `R-MAC-*` describe current experimental requirements where they agree with the authoritative profile. `R-STATE-*` and `R-ACCOUNT-*` preserve historical candidate scenarios and are non-normative. In `R-RESOURCE-*`, cases `006`–`009` remain applicable timeout checks; all other cases preserve the historical candidate unless independently restated by a current `R-OWNER-*` case. No historical row may reinstate multi-ceremony exposure, eight simultaneous responders, persistent `5,497` accounting, epoch reset, or the old SAS rate-control policy. Local-profile and adapter cases remain candidate-only. The per-pair argument remains conditional; no implementation verification has occurred.

> **P6 AMENDMENTS:** P6 amends existing rows in place and marks each amendment with its owner decision ID; it adds no row and renumbers nothing, so the current normative set is still the 92 rows the P4 conformance closure mapped. P6.1 amends `R-OWNER-023` with the connection lifetime selected by decision P6-D-001 ([P6 decisions](p6-remediation/decisions.md)). P6.2 amends `R-WIRE-025` and `R-MAC-012` with the graceful hang-up handling of decision P6-D-003 (bytes received before a peer's graceful end of stream stay ordinary input); P3 needed no normative change for it. P6.3 amends `R-OWNER-006`, `R-OWNER-017`, `R-OWNER-022`, `R-OWNER-031`, and `R-OWNER-038` under decision P6-D-002 to state explicitly what P3 already says: releasing and re-registering an authority inside the same live process is not a replacement process and starts no fresh budget or START limiter; P3 needed no normative change for it. P6.4 amends `R-OWNER-013` under decision P6-D-004 to state that an RNG panic or failure produces no exposure or result and does not reset the process session; ABI panic containment itself belongs to P7, and P3 needed no normative change. P6.5 amends `R-OWNER-007`, `R-MAC-012`, `R-RESOURCE-006`, and `R-RESOURCE-007` under decision P6-D-005 (local completion and the final-ACK deadline boundary, dispositioning P5-F-007): the Initiator's success is judged when its final-ACK send is confirmed, a deadline reached by then gives the Initiator no result even after a complete write, and the Responder's verified final ACK still gives the Responder its result, so either side may be the only result holder. P3 §9, §10, and §11.3 were clarified to say so, without changing the current behavior. Historical `R-STATE-006` is unchanged and stays non-normative.

Each row supplies a stable ID, precondition/input, expected outcome, and relevant accounting/resource boundary. `—` means the property does not apply. “Terminal” describes the active ceremony. For current behavior, use the authoritative profile and the applicable rows named above; historical rows are retained as decision evidence only.
