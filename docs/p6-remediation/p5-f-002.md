## 1. Finding

An admitted connection with no inbound frame in progress had no finite lifetime. P5 reproduced three subcases with one root cause:

- **A, first header:** admitted, zero bytes, held forever.
- **B, idle after its run:** a run ended, no new frame begins, held forever.
- **C, owner-less retained output:** the run is gone, an outbound frame (its timeout CANCEL, or a local REJECT's or CANCEL's frame) stays retained for a peer that never reads, held forever.

With `MAX_LIVE_UNAUTHENTICATED_CONNECTIONS = 16`, sixteen silent peers held every live slot of the authority indefinitely, at no ongoing cost.

## 2. Normative requirement

P3 §11.1.1 "Incoming connections" (the cap of 16 includes connections that have not sent a frame), "Incomplete frame reception" (finite header/whole-frame deadlines plus an idle-read deadline), and "Lifetime and cleanup" (temporary resources released on timeout); `R-OWNER-023`, `R-OWNER-026`. A finite bound was mandatory; its model and values were not frozen.

## 3. Chosen remediation

[P6-D-001](decisions.md#p6-d-001--f-002-connection-lifetime): a 10 s first-frame deadline, a 10 s quiescent deadline with no live run, and 10 s absolute / 2 s no-progress deadlines for owner-less retained output, with the existing incomplete-frame and ceremony deadlines unchanged and authoritative where they apply. Implementation and evidence follow in this record as they land.
